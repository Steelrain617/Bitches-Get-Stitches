A few common questions

How long does a custom piece take?

It depends on the piece and how many orders are ahead of yours. Once we talk through your idea, I'll give you an estimated timeline before you commit to anything.

Can I pick my own colors?

Yes. Most pieces in the gallery can be made in your choice of colors. Mention what you're picturing in your message and we'll work it out together.

Do you ship, and how much does it cost?

I can ship most pieces. Exact cost and pricing depend on the piece, size, and materials, so I'll confirm details with you directly once we've talked through your order.

What if I don't see what I want in the gallery?

That's exactly what custom orders are for. Send a message with your idea, even if it's just a rough sketch or a photo for inspiration.
